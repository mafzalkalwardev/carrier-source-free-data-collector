# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy toward other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others’ private information without permission
- Encouraging illegal activity or Terms-of-Service violations against third-party sites
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

Report concerns by opening a private security advisory or contacting the repository owner on GitHub.
